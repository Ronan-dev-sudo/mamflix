THE STORY BEGINS

CHAPTER ONE

The Woman.

Before the titles, the ministry and the moments that so many people have come to know, there is a woman.

A life, a heart, a journey and a beautiful story that deserves to be celebrated.

CHAPTER TWO

A Heart of Worship.

Dear Mam, I love how you bring stillness into the room through the essence of your worship.

If worship had a name, yours would be called worship.

I look up to you in the spaces of musicality because you are such a role model to me, Mam.

CHAPTER THREE

A Mother's Heart.

There is a kind of love that cannot be measured by words alone.

A mother's love. Her tenderness, her strength, her care and the beautiful place she holds in the lives of those she calls family.

CHAPTER FOUR

A Life That Touches Lives.

Some lives leave traces of love wherever they go.

Through the lives she has touched, the hearts she has encouraged and the people who carry something of her with them, her story continues.

CHAPTER FIVE

The Beauty of Her Journey.

Every season has held a lesson. Every chapter has carried its own beauty.

And every step has been part of a story still being written.

CHAPTER SIX

Letters to Mam.

Some things deserve to be said. Some appreciation deserves to be heard.

And so, these are words from hearts that are grateful for you.

With love, always.
